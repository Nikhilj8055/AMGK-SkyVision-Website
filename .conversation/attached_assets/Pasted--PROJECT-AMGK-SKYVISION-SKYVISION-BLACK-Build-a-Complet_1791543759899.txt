# PROJECT: AMGK SKYVISION — SKYVISION BLACK
## Build a Complete, Production-Quality Automotive Agency Website

Act as a senior creative director, award-winning UI/UX designer, motion designer, and senior React engineer.

Build a complete, visually exceptional, responsive website for **AMGK SkyVision**, an automotive content production and social media agency that primarily serves used-car dealerships.

This is Demo Concept 01 of two alternative website concepts. The design must look like a premium automotive film studio, not a generic marketing template.

The website must have a complete working frontend, all required pages, responsive layouts, functional interactions, reusable components, realistic content based on the supplied business information, and production-quality code.

Do not just produce a landing page, a wireframe, or a design description. Build the actual working application.
